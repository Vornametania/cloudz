cloudz_

// deep listening for altered states

- about

cloudz is a mix series built around long, uninterrupted listening. Ambient, experimental, techno, IDM, and bass music selected for stillness, focus, and states of mind that ordinary listening doesn't reach.

Every episode moves slowly and holds its shape. No sudden edges, no distractions, just sound meant to be entered rather than played in the background. It works for meditative states or for simply disappearing into something for a while.

Musicians are invited to submit mixes in this mode. Send your most inward work and let it reach the people who need it.

- manifesto

I didn't set out to start this series, but somewhere along the line I ended up here. Two years ago I was experimenting a lot with music and altered states at home alone. I learned a lot from that and this series is me wanting to share what I learned.

I understood that tracks that would soothe me during a regular afternoon turned chaotic in certain states, where my mind's walls were low. Some sounds could guide me to deeper meditation, while others would throw me off. Some created a safe room to just be in, without asking anything of me, and others kept me on alert and prevented surrender. During that time, by trial and error, I listened to a lot of music from different genres and artists.

I kept sharing what I was learning with friends, inviting them to join me at times, creating space for us to also listen together in full presence. I showed some of them how I personally enjoy combining altered states with music. Most people only know this in the form of big dancefloor rooms, fast paced techno, or the opposite, ambient to fall asleep to. But there's an in between. There are other ways to experience this that we're not often taught, or don't often share with each other.

I've watched friends experience music in this format for the first time. Find a safe place, preferably home or somewhere quiet outside, no one around unless you're doing it together. Lie down, play the music, close your eyes, let it do its job. Some of the people around me shared really beautiful experiences from this. Feeling total peace, close to the sun. Feeling light, like all their anxiety had lifted.

I found out later this wasn't something I'd just made up in my head. There's actual research on this, people at Imperial College London and Johns Hopkins have been studying music inside altered state work for years, and they kept landing on a lot of the same things I was noticing on my own. One of them even called music "the hidden therapist," because apparently in these states the music carries more of the weight than almost anything else happening in the room. It's not about how intense a track is or even what emotion it carries. It's the way it's put together.

So I started paying closer attention to what actually worked. Genre told me almost nothing, honestly. What mattered was structure. Long tones instead of short ones. Repetition that shifts instead of repetition that just loops. Vocals treated as texture, if they were there at all.

I'm not a researcher and this isn't a clinical thing. I just kept notes, every mix, every hour of listening that got it right for me, and at some point I realized I probably wasn't the only person doing this or looking for this.

This is for that person. cloudz is a place for music that was actually chosen. Not for the dancefloor, not for background focus but curated for going inward.
